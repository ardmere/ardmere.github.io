Minimum standard

01

Stage 0 is not effective PoR.

Incomplete or unverifiable disclosures do not meet the minimum evidentiary bar.

02

Stage 1 is the minimum effective threshold.

Effective PoR requires public evidence for reserves, liabilities, and key proofs.

03

Missing evidence is UNVERIFIABLE, not PASS.

Absence of evidence is recorded as a material gap, not treated as adequate proof.
