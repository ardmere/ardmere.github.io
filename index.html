Methodology

PoR Stage framework

Who must users ultimately trust? Every public rating is a Stage label—Pre-Stage through Stage 2—measuring how much disclosure removes reliance on exchange narrative alone.

Full methodology
Pre-Stage No usable PoR

No minimally usable snapshot, summary, or proof—or PoR has stopped. Users rely on brand, financial reports, or regulation, not reproducible PoR artifacts.

Stage 0 Trust the exchange

Has PoR artifacts, but third parties cannot independently reproduce global solvency. Summary or user inclusion primarily; key claims remain exchange narrative.

Stage 1 Verifiable disclosure

Public, reproducible reserves and liabilities—wallet_address_list, ownership proof, and global_proof. Minimum effective PoR for custody policy.

Stage 2 Trust-minimized PoR

Permissionless verification, business-aligned proof constraints, canonical anchoring, and compressed risk windows. Long-term industry target; no complete Stage 2 yet.

Standard

Effective PoR policy

Stage 1 is the minimum bar for effective PoR. Exchanges that custody customer assets must publish evidence third parties can reproduce—not marketing summaries alone. Stage 0 is transitional; financial audit and PoR are complementary, not interchangeable.
